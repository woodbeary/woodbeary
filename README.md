## Jacob Lopez

I build AI agents you can text, call, and wear. I shipped always-on agents you text in February 2026; xAI and OpenAI launched the same idea in August and September.

- **[TXT CLAW → Rotary](https://github.com/woodbeary/rotary)** (Feb–Apr 2026). A Claude agent for every user in its own Cloudflare Sandbox, reached by SMS and iMessage, then a native iPhone app where your agent makes the phone call and you steer it mid-call. Dated commits and architecture inside.
- **A real business on agents** (May 2026 to now). My own company's lead intake, triage, and replies run through agents, and nothing sends until a human approves it. Private; happy to walk through it.
